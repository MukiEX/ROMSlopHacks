Lords of Lunar — Paddle Patcher

For the Lunar: Silver Star Story Complete “Making of” disc (USA, SLUS_009.21). Adds PlayStation Mouse and NeGcon paddle control to the hidden game.

What it does

  • PS Mouse — horizontal movement slides the paddle around your castle like a spinner. Left click = ✕ (hold ball / confirm), right click = START in play (pause) and △ (back) in menus. In menus it works as a dial: moving left/right steps through characters, and through castle spots left-to-right, row by row.
  • NeGcon — the twist sets the paddle’s position directly across the castle’s open sides. Button I = ✕, II = □.
  • Digital and analog pads are unchanged. Works through a multitap.
  • Restart — hold L1+R1+L2+R2+START+SELECT on any pad to return to the title screen.
